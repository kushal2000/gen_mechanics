# Recentering: hand grammar for evolve-to-generalize (2026-10-05)

A one-page map for anyone starting a new session on this work. It covers what is settled, what works, what is blocked, and how to split the work. Details live in `STATE.json` (issues and settled decisions), `LOG.md` (chronology) and `isaacsimenvs/inhand_reorient/evolution/README.md` (methods and results tables). Branch: `martin/hand-grammar`; never touch other branches.

## 1. The goal
We are building a generative grammar for robot-hand kinematics (LocoFormer-inspired). Evolution searches it for diverse hand designs that a single shared controller can drive, ideally zero-shot on commercial hands (Allegro, LEAP, SHARPA, Wuji, XHand, DClaw, Tesollo). The grammar must be:
- expressive enough to cover real hands;
- searchable;
- able to keep diversity, so that no single root hand dominates;
- tractable, so that evolution does not collapse into tiny refinements.

## 2. The grammar (the core of this project)

### Representation (settled; do not reopen)
- `KinematicModel`: palm bodies plus digits made of capsule links, revolute joints with free SE(3) origins, one motor per joint, no wrists.
- Prismatic and coupled joints are future items.
- It reproduces 14 real hands exactly (Pinocchio cross-checks). Capsules are the collision geometry.
- Code: `hand_sampler/grammar/` (`derive.py`, `distributions.py`, `variants.py`, `fk.py`, `geometry.py`, `adapters/`); tests in `hand_sampler/grammar_bench/` (3157 pass).

### Generative rules and variants
| Variant | Definition |
|---|---|
| G_SERIAL | old-sampler-like |
| DEFAULT | grammar 0.5 |
| G_V1 | DEFAULT constrained to simulator-compatible hands |
| G_V1S | + digits mounted on the host surface |
| G_V2S | + cross-host mount spacing |
| G_V3S | + curl-axis and opposition priors (host-frame fixed) |

Mutation uses `derive.vary` with `EVOLUTION_OPERATORS` (exact-inverse pairs); immigrants also exist. All variants are off-by-default additions, and old variants are byte-identical.

### Grammar evidence so far

| Measure | V1 | V1S | V2S | V3S |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CPU viability, 2000 seeds: admitted (no rest overlap at q0 and reset pose) and ≥ 2 fingertips reach the spawn | 1.95% | 3.05% | 5.0% | 9.35% |
| Descriptor cells filled (of 30) | 11 | 12 | 14 | 15 |
| Physics viability under HORA (stable grasp found, opposition-seeded search) | 4.3% | – | 5.2% | 12.4% |

Report: `experiments/g0/report_v2.md`.
- **Trade-off:** the curl prior reduces continuous shape diversity (164 → 115 mm).
- **Offspring of viable parents** are about 66% viable.
- **Evolution on the legacy task with an MLP controller** (pilots 2-3; historical only): median elite fitness ranks V3S > V2S > V1; coverage is similar (about 12 of 30 cells); founder dominance is moderate (max share 0.2-0.36).

## 3. Simulation and task (`isaacsimenvs/inhand_reorient/`)
- **Scene:** a stationary palm-up hand, no arm.
- **Task profiles:** `legacy`, `isaaclab_repose`, `anyrotate`, and **`hora`** (current).
  - HORA (Qi et al., CoRL 2022) is ported from its released code: z-axis rotation reward, a 1.5 cm fall termination and 20 s episodes.
- **Grammar → simulator:** a 32-slot padded envelope with palm carriers; FK matches to 4e-7 m; a viability oracle; a HORA-style grasp cache; `--viable-only` populations.
- **Key physical finding:** the starting grasp decides learnability.
  - Allegro learns HORA (about 14 s holding, about 2 rotations per episode) only with grasps seeded from HORA's hand-tuned pose.
  - Grammar hands, and even sharpa and leap, either flick and drop the object (no drop cost) or hold still without rotating (drop penalty).

## 4. The controller
- **The team's per-joint token transformer is THE multi-hand policy** (Martin, 2026-10-04). It is ported as `token_policy.py` and `token_layout.py`, with the raw enabled mask and valid-token normalisation.
- **Recipe R2** (the collaborator's recipe: lr 1e-4 adaptive, e_clip 0.1, 2 mini-epochs, d_model 128, SAPG with 6 blocks, about 12k envs) is required.
  - Solo allegro: 15.1 s / 2.46 rotations per episode, beating the MLP (1.91 rotations).
  - It needs about 4× the MLP's samples and roughly 6 h runs.
- **Two hands at once (allegro + founder 174):** works. Allegro reaches its solo level at about solo per-hand samples. Per-design advantage normalisation (vendored rl_games, approved, `e4dee41`) makes it turn earlier. Founder 174 matches its own solo level, but that level is a flick.
- **GET-Zero-style distillation** (per-hand MLP experts → one transformer by DAgger) works: the student reaches 94-105% of each expert. It is limited by the experts: only allegro's expert holds and rotates.

## 5. Evolution infrastructure
- **Driver** (`isaacsimenvs/inhand_reorient/evolution/driver.py`):
  - a MAP-Elites archive over digits × joint-count bins (30 cells);
  - probe hands;
  - weights carried between generations;
  - retries and finite-checkpoint checks;
  - grasp cache, viable-only populations and a spare pool;
  - the transformer is the default for the hora profile.
- **Analysis:** `experiments/evolution_pilot/analyze_pilot.py`.
- **Cluster:**
  - Jobs run from per-commit code snapshots (`code/gm_<sha>`, `PYTHONPATH` shadowing).
  - At most 4 GPUs on the main tier is the lab norm.
  - About 210 GPU-h used so far.
  - The ssh master drops periodically; Martin reconnects it, and agents must never log in.

## 6. Open problems, in dependency order
1. **P1. A task and training recipe where arbitrary (grammar) hands learn to hold AND rotate.** This blocks every performance-based grammar comparison and evolution run.
   - Ready to test but never run: enclosing (gait-supporting) grasp acceptance and drop-penalty schedules (`41761e4`).
   - Other options: change the task (e.g. a reward that pays rotation only while held); per-hand grasp design; R2 transformer experts.
2. **P2. Shared transformer at scale:** 4 and 8 hands with R2, with and without per-design advantage normalisation. This has not run yet; the queue died with the session.
   - Jobs still running at writing time: pair resumes 2558713_21 and 2558714_22 (end about 19:15) and solo 416 2559626_6. Results go to `runs/viable/fair_*`.
3. **P3. Grammar evaluation that does not wait for P1:** kinematic and grasp-based viability, coverage, diversity and evolvability. Already discriminative (V3S 12.4% vs V1 4.3% physics viability). Could be extended: does a grasp exist that supports finger gaiting? Is the workspace sufficient for rotation? How large is the grasp set?
4. **P4. Evolution with the transformer across grammar variants.** Needs P1 and P2.

## 7. Suggested split into sessions
Each session reads this file, STATE.json and the README first. Each commits only its own paths and keeps its own log under `project-notes/grammar/sessions/<session>.md`, to avoid note conflicts.

Respect a combined cluster cap of 4 GPUs:
- A: 0;
- B: ≤ 2;
- C: ≤ 2;
- D: later, when B and C stop.

**A. Grammar (back to basics; mostly CPU).** Owns `hand_sampler/grammar/`, `experiments/grammar_screen/` and `project-notes/grammar/experiments/g0/`.
- Refine the generative rules using the viability failure modes.
- Add grammar metrics that predict manipulability without RL (gait-supporting grasps, rotation workspace, opposition).
- Keep the representation settled.
- Write up the grammar story.

**B. Task and experts (P1).** Owns `isaacsimenvs/inhand_reorient/` task, reward and grasp code.
- Make grammar hands hold and rotate.
- Then rerun the GET-Zero round-2 pipeline (scripts in the old scratchpad `r2/` folder; the README "Round 2" section).

**C. Shared transformer (P2).** Owns `token_policy.py`, the train YAMLs and the fair-test tools.
- Collect the running pair and solo 416 results.
- Run 4 and 8 hands with R2 (± per-design advantage normalisation).
- Then wire R2 into the evolution driver defaults.

**D. Evolution (P4), after B and C.** Runs the grammar comparison under evolution with the transformer.
